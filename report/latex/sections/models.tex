Two model families are used for each problem, each starting from its simplest
variant as a baseline. All preprocessing is part of the scikit-learn pipeline,
and the random seed is fixed at 42.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering

        \hline
        \textbf{Family} & \textbf{Problem A (classification)} & \textbf{Problem B (regression)} \\
        \hline
        Linear & Logistic regression (baseline), L1 logistic regression & Linear regression (baseline), Ridge, Lasso \\
        \hline
        Tree-based & Decision tree (baseline), random forest \todo{or boosting} & Decision tree (baseline), random forest \todo{or boosting} \\
        \hline
        Reference & Majority class / no-skill & Median of training \texttt{delivery\_days} \\
        \hline
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
