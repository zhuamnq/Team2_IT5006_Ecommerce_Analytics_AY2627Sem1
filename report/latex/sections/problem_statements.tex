Building on the candidate problems identified in Milestone 1, this phase
addresses two delivery-related prediction problems on the Olist dataset. All
orders in the dataset are domestic Brazilian deliveries; 63.8\% of order items
are shipped across state borders. Both problems predict at the moment of
purchase, so only information available at that time is used as input. The
two formulations follow the delivery-prediction literature reviewed in
Milestone 1: late-delivery classification and delivery-time regression
\citep{steinberg2023late, salari2022delivery}.

\subsection{Problem A: Late Delivery Prediction (Classification)}

\textbf{Question.} Will an order be delivered after the estimated delivery date
shown to the customer at purchase?

\textbf{Target.} \texttt{is\_late} $= 1$ if the calendar date of
\texttt{order\_\allowbreak delivered\_\allowbreak customer\_\allowbreak date} is later than
\texttt{order\_\allowbreak estimated\_\allowbreak delivery\_\allowbreak date}, and $0$ otherwise. Dates rather than
timestamps are compared because the estimated date is stored as midnight.
6.8\% of delivered orders are late.

\textbf{Stakeholder.} \todo{e.g.\ Olist logistics and customer-service teams,
who could flag high-risk orders early. Milestone 1 showed late orders average a
2.57 review score against 4.29 for on-time orders.}

\textbf{Success criteria.} \todo{e.g.\ PR-AUC above the no-skill baseline of
0.068, and recall of at least X\% at precision of at least Y\%.}

\subsection{Problem B: Delivery Time Prediction (Regression)}

\textbf{Question.} How many days will an order take to reach the customer?

\textbf{Target.} \texttt{delivery\_days}, the time from
\texttt{order\_\allowbreak purchase\_\allowbreak timestamp} to \texttt{order\_\allowbreak delivered\_\allowbreak customer\_\allowbreak date}
in days.

\textbf{Stakeholder.} \todo{e.g.\ Olist, to set more accurate delivery
promises; sellers, to plan dispatch.}

\textbf{Success criteria.} \todo{e.g.\ MAE below the naive median baseline and
below the error of Olist's own estimated delivery date.}
